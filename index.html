About Me

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Caltech, minoring in Aerospace Engineering and Robotics, with a passion for automation, controls, and turning ideas into working hardware. My experience spans factory automation at Dynamic Motion Control, robotics research at Northwestern's McCormick School of Engineering, and CNC machining instruction at Caltech's ME13/113 lab.

Robotic Gripper

Designed and fabricated a specialized robotic gripper integrated with custom sensors to enable advanced robotic machine learning models, as part of research at Northwestern's McCormick School of Engineering analyzing ergodic robotic control and controlled diffusion.

RC Rocker-Bogie Rover

Engineered a rocker-bogie suspension system to traverse rough terrain with minimal body vibration, integrating a 4-DOF robotic arm with a custom gripper, R/C-controlled through Arduino and dual 45A RoboClaw motor controllers.

Bike Wheel Transmission

Co-designed and fabricated a custom belt-drive transmission housed in a laser-cut acrylic gearbox under a $200 budget, optimizing timing belts, pulleys, and shafts for maximum power transfer and efficiency — placing 4th overall out of all competing teams.
